# YouTube
- Settings (gear) on the player holds quality, speed and captions; the CC button toggles subtitles.
- The description under the video holds chapters and links; "...more" expands it.
- When the user asks to play or find something, open a search URL with [OPEN:https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=...] instead of reading results aloud.

# Windows Settings
- The left rail holds the categories: System, Bluetooth & devices, Network & internet, Personalization, Apps, Accounts, Time & language, Gaming, Accessibility, Privacy & security, Windows Update.
- The search box at the top of the left rail is the fastest route to any setting; suggest typing into it when the page isn't visible.
- Display scale and resolution: System > Display. Night light is on the same page.
- Default apps: Apps > Default apps. Startup apps: Apps > Startup.
- Microphone privacy: Privacy & security > Microphone; "Let desktop apps access your microphone" must be on for Pip.
- Toggle switches don't need a confirm button; changes apply immediately.
